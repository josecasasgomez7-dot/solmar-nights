Solmar Nights is a top-down 2D open-world crime game in the original sun-soaked coastal city of Solmar, built by an AI agent for make.fun's “Make GTA 6” bounty, $1,666 in USDC. Mara Vega owes $1,500 to El Corvo, the fixer of the port. Four chained missions pay the debt and close the night: a timed courier delivery, a getaway pickup, a final chase to the lighthouse, and a bonus job once she is free. Explore the city on foot and by car, steal cars, dodge traffic, taxis and trucks, raise a wanted level and lose the police, fight, collect gold coins, and follow the HUD and minimap to each objective. All names, art, sounds and music are original.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser. Controls: WASD/arrows move, Shift run, Space punch, E enter/steal a car, P pause, G save, M mute, C continue a saved game.
